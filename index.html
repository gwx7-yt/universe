Scientific Basis

How CosmoScope Connects to Real Astronomy

Scale, Light, and Distance

Astronomers study the universe by comparing scale, measuring light, and using distance units such as kilometers, astronomical units, light-years, and megaparsecs.

Stellar Evolution

Stars evolve differently depending on mass. More massive stars burn fuel faster, shine brighter, and can end as neutron stars or black holes after supernova explosions.

Gravity and Black Holes

Gravity depends on mass and distance. A black hole's event horizon is the boundary where escape velocity is greater than the speed of light.
